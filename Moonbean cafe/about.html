About Our Cafe

MoonBean Cafe started as a tiny corner cafe filled with coffee aroma, fairy lights, and midnight playlists. Today, it has become a cozy place where students study, friends laugh for hours, and tired people recharge their batteries with warm drinks.

Every cup here is brewed with care. Our chefs prepare desserts fresh every morning, turning ordinary mornings into edible poetry.
